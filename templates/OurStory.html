They say

"Marry Your Best Friend"

WE COULDN'T AGREE MORE.

Our story began at sixteen, with a first date in Rome and a first kiss in Venice just two days later. It sounds grand on paper, but to us, it simply feels like the most natural, fitting start to our life together.

But the foundation wasn’t built in Italy; it was built in the back of a classroom. Long before the school trip, we were the friends constantly getting dirty looks for talking too much; debating personality test results, swapping favorite music, and getting distracted by each other when we were supposed to be taking notes. We were then, and remain now, the kind of couple who talk nonstop, laugh at inside jokes, and genuinely love being in each other’s orbit.

Since then, we’ve done what growing up together requires: long distance, college, and a move to Virginia to start our careers. None of it has been easy, but we’ve learned that life feels lighter when you’re on the same team.

As we count down the days to our marriage, we hope that we keep loving each other the way we always have: attentively, generously, and with deep joy. As we grow and change, we can’t wait to fall in love with the new versions of each other, again and again.
