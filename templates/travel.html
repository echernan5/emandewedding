Travel & Transit

Ensuring your journey to us is seamless and comfortable is our greatest wish.

The Journey

We recommend flying into Grand Rapids (GRR) or Kalamazoo (AZO). For a scenic arrival from Chicago, the Amtrak Wolverine Line to Kalamazoo is a guest favorite.

If you are driving, Bay Pointe Inn is a pleasant route from the city. Complimentary parking is available property-wide upon your arrival.

Wedding Day

Shuttle Service: A private shuttle will run continuously between Best Western, Gun Lake Casino, and the venue throughout the evening.

Local Guests: For those driving or staying locally, ample complimentary parking is available directly at the Lakefront Pavilion.
