Travel & Transit

For our guests traveling from near and far.

Getting to Michigan

The closest airports are Grand Rapids (GRR) and Kalamazoo (AZO). If you are coming from Chicago, the Amtrak Wolverine Line to Kalamazoo is also a great travel option.

If you are driving, Shelbyville is a pleasant route from Illinois. We recommend stopping in New Buffalo for lunch and shopping on your way.

Wedding Day

Shuttle Service: A private motorcoach will run continuously between Best Western, Gun Lake Casino, and the venue throughout the evening.

On-Site Parking: Plenty of complimentary parking is available on-site.
