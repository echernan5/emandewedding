The Essentials

Everything you need to know for the big weekend.

What time should I arrive?

Seating opens at 4:00 PM, and the ceremony will begin at 4:30 PM.

Where will the ceremony and reception take place?

Both will take place at Bay Pointe Inn’s Lakefront Pavilion (11456 Marsh Rd, Shelbyville, MI 49344).

Will the ceremony be outdoors?

Weather permitting, yes — we plan to celebrate on the lawn by the lake.

What should I wear?

Cocktail attire. Suits (ties optional), midi or tea-length dresses, dressy separates, or a jumpsuit are all welcome. Colorful attire is encouraged.

Any shoe recommendations?

Because the ceremony will be held on the lawn, block heels, wedges, or formal flats tend to be the most comfortable choice.

What should I expect in terms of weather?

The Pavilion is open-air but equipped with fans, heaters, and drop-down walls to suit the forecast.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes. Complimentary motorcoach transportation will run continuously throughout the evening. Pickup details will be shared closer to the date.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — complimentary on-site parking is available directly in front of the Inn.

Are Uber/Lyft available?

They can be, but availability may be limited later in the evening — planning ahead is a good idea if you’re staying off-site.

Should we plan to attend the welcome gathering?

Only if it fits your travel plans. It’s a relaxed option for guests who happen to be arriving on Thursday, and we’d truly love to see you if you’re in town. There’s absolutely no expectation to travel early or adjust plans in order to attend.

Will more details be shared closer to the weekend?

Yes — as plans finalize, we’ll post timing/location updates on the website.
