Our Story

They say "Marry your best friend"

We couldn’t agree more. We’re the kind of couple who talk nonstop, laugh at things only we find funny, and genuinely love being around each other. We’re partners in the truest sense: authentic, loving, and effortlessly in sync. After six years together, that partnership still feels so right.

As we count down the days to our marriage, we hope it keeps feeling like this. That we continue loving each other wholeheartedly, easily, and with unending joy. We can't wait to continue to fall in love with the new versions of each other, again and again.
